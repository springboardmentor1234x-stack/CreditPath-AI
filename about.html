About CreditPathAI

Mission: Reduce defaults by providing agents with prioritized, actionable recovery steps.

How AI predicts risk

A machine-learning model trained on historical borrower data outputs a probability of default. The app maps that probability to risk tiers using percentile thresholds.

Recommendations mapping

  • Low: Standard Reminder
  • Medium: Personalized Call / Email
  • High: Priority Collection / Restructure
